Library / Books for developing readers

A reviewed reading list

Books that build what children know

Knowledge-rich books for developing readers, from picture books to books for older teens. Each has a short review from the teacher, educator or parent who recommended it.

Most are nonfiction that children enjoy, such as comic-book histories. The novels teach something real too, such as what happened during a historical event. Many are books you may not know yet.

Stories, poems and myths show what it means to be human. We include enduring fables because they shape our civic life, and we do not shy away from tough topics.
